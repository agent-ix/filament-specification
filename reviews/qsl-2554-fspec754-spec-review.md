## Summary

Spec review of FSpec PR #754. Ticket: QSL-2554. The PR adds a claim-member production to FR-322 "Body grammar" for `claim`/`probabilistic_claim` bodies, capped at four aggregates nested below the body aggregate, spells it in the V2 schema as five non-recursive aggregate levels, and adds FR-322-AC-483 with TC-427 row BG-06 and a Rust schema test. It also adds a "Refusal locus" paragraph to FR-640 rules 6 and 7 and FR-640-AC-12 with TC-1563 step 10, which pin nine refusal pointers. The review checked the nesting against FR-640's nine bindings, recounted every pointer, checked the schema routing, ran the targeted test, checked ids against main and open PRs, and read filament-contract-ir PR #1077.

## Examined scope

- Nesting depth (examined). The deepest chain is body aggregate (level 0), `form` (1), `event` (2), compare `measure` (3), fraction `weighting` (4), whose members are Leaf bindings. Other chains are shallower: `scheduler` to `fairness` to member reaches 3, compare `bound` reaches 3, quantile `measure` and `long_run_fraction` `weighting` reach 2. So the cap of four admits every FR-640 body. It also refuses nothing FR-640 defines.
- Pointer counts (examined). The weighting `kind` literal is at `/semantic_graph/nodes/{i}/body/members/5/value/members/1/value/members/1/value/members/3/value/members/0/value`, 19 steps, and its `value` member is at 20. The compare measure's `from` target `.../members/1/value/target` is at 17. contract-ir #1077 raises `Path::CAPACITY` to 20, which holds 20. Its comment and FR-377 text still say 17.
- FR-640-AC-12 pointers (examined). All nine match FR-640's binding order: `reward` index 2, `distributions` index 3, `subject` index 0 `/value/target`, `scheduler` index 1 `/value/target`, the duplicated `fairness` element `members/1/value/members/0/value/members/1`, the quantile `form` `kind` `members/5/value/members/0/value`, the quantile measure (form member 2) `from` (measure member 1) target, and the `activation` (index 7) `trigger` (member 1) target. They agree with CL-64 (refusal at the binding's value). The AC can fail, because it names the binding node and the bare reference term as wrong loci.
- Schema routing (examined). `BodyBindingRules` branch 10 matches `node_tag` `claim` with `semantic_form` `probabilistic_claim` only. Every other node keeps `SemanticTerm`. `ProbabilisticClaimAggregate3` admits `GroupTerm`, so five aggregate definitions cover levels 0-4. No definition refers to itself, so the "JSON depth fixed by the schema" invariant holds. No existing fixture holds a probabilistic claim graph node (only `checked_term_data` rows), so no other vectors change.
- Rust test (examined). `cargo test --test checked_package_v2` passes the new test. It checks the admitted deepest shape, the pointer, generic-Body refusal, `verification_claim` refusal, the five-deep aggregate, binding-of-binding, inline application and Leaf root.
- Ids (examined). FR-322-AC-483, FR-640-AC-12, TC-427 BG-06 and TC-1563 step 10 are free on origin/main (cdd6cbeb) and in open PRs #753, #695 and #640. The planned FR-322-AC-480 renumber to AC-484 does not collide.
- IR follow-ups (examined). The PR body and the QSL-2554 ruling comment state two of them: refuse deeper nesting and binding-of-binding as `malformed_wire`, and 19 steps rather than 17. See FND-003 for the one that is missing.

## Verdict

Mergeable after fixing the two medium findings. The grammar, cap, schema, pointer counts, the nine AC-12 pointers and the ids are correct. FND-001 (medium): the locus paragraph contradicts FR-640-AC-11's spelling refusal at a literal's `value` member. FND-002 (medium): FR-322-AC-483's reader clauses have no runner. FND-003, FND-004 and FND-005 are low.
